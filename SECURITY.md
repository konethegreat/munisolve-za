# Security Policy

## Supported Versions

We are committed to securing the MuniSolveZA platform. Security updates are prioritized for the latest stable releases.


## Reporting a Vulnerability

If you discover a security vulnerability within MuniSolveZA, please do **not** open a public issue. Instead, follow the process below:

### How to report
Please send an email to **erictshivhinda@gmail.com** (or your designated project email) with the following details:
* **Type of issue** (e.g., XSS, SQL Injection, Unauthorized Access).
* **Location** (e.g., specific API endpoint or UI component).
* **Step-by-step instructions** to reproduce the issue.
* **Potential impact** if the vulnerability is exploited.

### What to expect
* **Acknowledgement:** You will receive an initial response within **48 hours** confirming that we have received your report.
* **Updates:** We will provide status updates at least once every **7 days** while the issue is being investigated and remediated.
* **Resolution:** Once a fix is verified, we will coordinate a disclosure date with you.
* **Recognition:** With your permission, we would be happy to credit you in our release notes for helping keep the community safe.

### Responsible Disclosure Guidelines
To protect our users and the integrity of municipal data, we ask that you:
1.  Give us a reasonable amount of time to fix the issue before making any information public.
2.  Avoid accessing or modifying data that does not belong to you.
3.  Do not perform Denial of Service (DoS) attacks or social engineering against MuniSolveZA staff or users.
